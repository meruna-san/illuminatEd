PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how IlluminatEd collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your email address and username.
  • User Content: Tasks, goals, journal entries, and focus session data you create.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics (via PostHog) to understand how the app is used.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve the app, personalize your experience, and communicate with you. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL). We implement Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure that only you can access your personal tasks, journal entries, and stats. Your data is not visible to other users.

4. Analytics

We use PostHog for analytics. We have configured PostHog to mask all text and input attributes, meaning we do not record your passwords, journal entries, or personal task descriptions. We only see anonymous clicks and session interactions.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, edit, or delete your data at any time. You can delete your account and all associated data via the Settings page (Wipe Data).

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at getilluminated.org@gmail.com.
